Confidence-Interval Model

Rationale (read-only)

1. Pull player game-logs this season + last-two-season vs same opponent.
2. Build 90 % t-interval around the stat mean.
3. Convert FanDuel price → implied probability.
4. Edge = (1-implied) – P(stat ≥ line).
5. Rank by edge; take top 5 per game.
